System Architecture

Four specialised LLM agents form a council, each assigned to one evidence tier (T1–T4). Verdicts are reached via a structured two-round debate governed by an axiom hierarchy.

Ablation Study

Seven variants tested on the same 30 cases to quantify each component's contribution. Results show component removal impact relative to full system.

AUROC by Variant
Higher is better. Full system = 0.811 baseline.
Cohen's κ and Coverage
κ = agreement beyond chance; coverage = % decisive.
no_debate — cov=13%
Without the debate engine, agents almost always abstain (only 4/30 decisive). The council protocol is essential for confident verdicts.
no_genomics — AUROC=0.614
T1 is the single most important agent. Removing it drops AUROC by 0.197 and κ collapses to 0.097 — somatic mutation data is irreplaceable.
no_pathway — AUROC=0.663
After pathway fixes (activity filter + z≥1.0 threshold), T3 contributes meaningfully. AUROC drops 0.148 without it, validating the engineering work.
no_transcriptomics — AUROC=0.705
T2 provides meaningful discriminative signal. Expression z-scores distinguish active-target cell lines from inactive ones and complement T1 mutation data.
no_axioms — AUROC=0.840
Removing the axiom hierarchy slightly improves AUROC but drops coverage to 83%. The hierarchy over-constrains in some cases — room for tuning threshold values.
no_pharmacology — AUROC=0.591 ↓ (worst)
Removing T4 (GDSC2 IC50) is the most damaging ablation: AUROC collapses to 0.591, κ drops to −0.026 (near-random agreement), and coverage falls to 70%. Pharmacology drove 16/30 decisive verdicts — it is the single most load-bearing agent in the current case mix.

Baseline: All ablations compared to full_system (AUROC=0.811, κ=0.375, coverage=93%). All 7 variants complete.

Engineering Fixes Implemented

Three targeted fixes applied after root-cause analysis of false-RESISTANT predictions in the pathway agent.

🔍
Pathway Activity Gate

Previously, the pathway agent considered all KEGG pathways containing a drug target — including neurological, reproductive, and metabolic pathways irrelevant to a cancer cell line. This generated spurious bypass routes.

Fix: for each candidate pathway, compute the fraction of pathway member genes expressed at z≥0.5 in the cell line. Skip the pathway if <40% of genes are active.

📊
Bypass Expression Threshold z≥1.0

The original bypass gene filter used z>0 (expressed above the gene's mean across cell lines). This passed ~39% of all measurements — far too permissive, generating false bypass routes.

Fix: raise threshold to z≥1.0 (notably upregulated, 1 standard deviation above mean). Both thresholds derived from gene-normalised z-scores: z = (expr − gene_mean) / gene_std.

⚖️
T3 Quorum Rule

Previously, the pathway agent could force a RESISTANT verdict alone even when both T1 (genomics) and T2 (transcriptomics) were UNCERTAIN. This caused false positives from bypass detection without corroborating evidence.

Fix: if T1 AND T2 are both UNCERTAIN, demote T3 from the decisive pool. It cannot form a consensus alone — the case escalates to the axiom resolver instead.

T1 = UNCERTAIN
T2 = UNCERTAIN
T3 = RESISTANT ← sole decisive
→ Quorum fails → escalate to resolver
Limitations & Next Steps

Known limitations and planned improvements for the full 100-case validation.

  • Pathway database (KEGG) is not cancer-specific. Integration of OmniPath cancer signalling atlas is planned to replace generic pathway lookups with tumour-context curated networks.
  • T3 quorum rule correctly improves net accuracy (+2 cases) but can suppress valid pathway-only resistance signals (e.g., J82+CHIR-99021, HeLa+Navitoclax). Threshold tuning needed.
  • Pharmacology (T4) was the winning agent in 16/30 cases, suggesting the system relies heavily on historical IC50 data. Performance on novel drugs with sparse GDSC2 coverage may degrade.
  • Hallucination rate and biological faithfulness scores require evidence logging infrastructure not yet implemented — planned for next iteration.
  • Coverage drops to 13% without the debate engine (no_debate ablation), revealing that LLM agents are often too cautious to commit individually. Confidence calibration work required.
  • Full 100-case validation (5 sets × 20 cases) not yet run with current fixes. Mini-validation results (n=30) may not fully represent performance on harder distribution tail cases.
